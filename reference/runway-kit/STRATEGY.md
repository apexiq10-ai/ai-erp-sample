# Rillet: the ledger that shows its work

## The decision

Build the launch story around **proof**, with the month-end close as the first use case. “AI-native ERP” is the category label. It does not explain why a skeptical finance team should switch its general ledger. A current, traceable close can.

**Positioning:** For finance teams outgrowing their ERP, Rillet keeps the books current and makes each number traceable to its source, so the close becomes confirmation instead of reconstruction. The controller remains responsible for review and sign-off.

**Brand idea:** The ledger that shows its work.

**Buyer promise:** Always current. Always provable.

**Mechanism:** Connect. Reason. Prove.

**Next action:** Bring your last close.

## Who this experience is for

The primary audience is Rillet leadership and a hiring panel evaluating a Director of Product Marketing proposal. The secondary audience is a finance buyer viewing the concept as an example of future campaign expression. The site presents a proposed strategy, not an official Rillet product site.

The intended 12-minute route is: the problem → the value → the close → the proof chain → the buyers → GTM sequence → measurement → next action. Scrolling supports self-guided reading; Present mode supports a live walkthrough.

## Creative system

The visual language turns accounting work into spatial evidence. The opening 3D month moves continuously until the viewer freezes it into a month-end ridge. The close workbench contrasts serial reconstruction with a continuous, human-reviewed workflow. The core sample descends through one number's evidence layers. Motion illustrates the argument, not a claim of live product telemetry.

The opening month uses code-generated 3D graphics so the viewer can turn and freeze it. The two priority scenes also use packaged Runway motion as atmospheric background, with all copy and controls in code. The footage is explicitly illustrative and must never be presented as product or customer data.

**Visual tone:** near-black, graphite, silver, and warm white. Rillet purple (`#644EFF`) marks only interaction and a few evidence points; deep violet (`#240257`) sits in shadows. Full-bleed 3D Runway motion and monumental type follow an editorial composition inspired by Colossal, while remaining an original Rillet proposal. Space Grotesk and DM Sans follow current public-site cues. The typographic prototype mark is a stand-in until an approved Rillet logo is supplied.

## Copy rules

- Be direct, dry, and specific. Aim the edge at reconstruction and legacy process, never at accountants.
- Name the human decision. “The machine prepares. The human signs.”
- Use the words finance teams use: close, tie-out, source, exception, policy, sign-off.
- Avoid guaranteed zero-day closes, “audit-proof,” headcount-reduction promises, and broad “AI-powered” headlines.
- Do not publish a numerical outcome before the customer, timeframe, and measurement method are verified.
- Keep provenance close to the claim. The interactive sample is explicitly illustrative.

## Launch system

| Phase | Main work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harvest | Interview customers and controllers; collect 20 to 30 close baselines; map objections; test messaging | Approved proof library and validated claims |
| Prove | Run a scoped Parallel Close; involve the controller, implementation lead, and auditor early | Repeatable tie-out to signed numbers and documented exceptions |
| Launch | CEO point of view; State of the Close; customer stories; seller and auditor evidence kits | Qualified evaluation movement, not just reach |
| Scale | Vertical plays, CPA partners, Close Club, paid, lifecycle, and advocacy | Cost per qualified Parallel Close start improves for two consecutive months |

### Parallel Close offer

A closed period is replayed on Rillet in a defined scope. Rillet and the prospect agree on a controller sponsor, time commitment, data inputs, comparison method, success criteria, and commercial terms before the work begins. The outcome is compared with already signed numbers, including any differences or exceptions. Product and Implementation must confirm feasibility before this is marketed as an offer.

### Channel priorities

1. Customer proof and profession: customer references, CPA firms, auditor evidence package.
2. Owned thought leadership: CEO point of view, State of the Close, Close Notes.
3. Trigger-led demand: ERP renewal, fundraise, new entity, first major audit, SOX or IPO planning.
4. Selective paid and events once the Parallel Close motion converts.

Never schedule controller outreach during days 1 to 7 of a month by default. Validate actual close calendars by account.

## Measurement

**Marketing north star:** qualified Parallel Close starts. **Customer-value north star:** median days to close. Set targets only after baseline collection.

Track four linked channels of evidence: use, value, trust, and commercial movement. Combine self-reported attribution with CRM touches and stage velocity. Review starts and stage friction weekly; close outcomes, conversion, and message pull-through monthly; revenue, win rate, and budget allocation quarterly. Stop a program that gains attention without meaningful stage movement after 90 days.

## Evidence and launch gates

The attachment mixes company statements, inference, illustrative visuals, and hypotheses. This site keeps those categories separate. Before external publication, verify:

- Current Rillet terminology, product workflow, access controls, and approval mechanics.
- Customer permissions and the exact basis of every quantified outcome.
- Whether a closed period can be replayed, at what cost, and with which implementation resources.
- Auditor and partner participation in a formal evidence package.
- Brand fit, legal review, and competitor claims.
