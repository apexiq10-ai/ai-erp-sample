# Runway 3D motion files

Two cinematic, 3D-rendered, silent Runway films are packaged locally for the Rillet concept. They are the visual centerpiece; the words and interactions are rendered in HTML and JavaScript.

| Heroics don't scale | `embed/heroics-ledger-monolith.mp4` | `98290f42-feb5-4610-a31f-a55aa2cd7f88` | Five suspended graphite and silver ledger strata with connected white filaments and a few violet points |
| Bring your last close | `embed/bring-your-last-close.mp4` | `61f13fb4-7e68-4b69-9e8a-5d1641ea656e` | Monumental graphite slabs and a precise evidence line, aligning in a black studio void |

Each file is approximately ten seconds, 1920 × 1080, H.264, muted, and intended as a full-bleed loop. The close clip was cropped and reframed slightly to remove a small AI-generated pseudo-label at its bottom edge. The clean packaged MP4 is the one to use. The matching JPEG stills are for loading, reduced-motion, and failure states.

The films are **illustrative art**, not Rillet product footage, customer data, or a claim about a specific workflow. Rillet purple appears only as a few points of light and in interface accents. Do not put copy or controls into the video itself.

## Creative prompts

**Heroics:** A monumental graphite ledger monolith suspended in a cavernous near-black void; five enormous strata of dark glass and brushed silver; slow descending camera orbit; cool-white filaments connecting each layer; tiny electric-violet junction points; dramatic physical scale, left-side negative space, no words or UI.

**Bring your last close:** Thirty tall graphite ledger slabs in a vast black studio; loose silver threads aligning into one precise evidence plane; two parallel structures settling into registration; slow forward camera; one small electric-violet approval point; restrained black, charcoal, and silver composition, left-side negative space, no words or UI.
